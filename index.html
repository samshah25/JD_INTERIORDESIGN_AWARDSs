COMPETITION RULES

Important Guidelines & Eligibility Rules

Please review the rules carefully before submitting your entry.

1

Deadline & Results Schedule

Entries must be submitted by 31st August, 2026 (11:59 PM IST). Results will be announced on 10 September 2026, and winners will be notified via personal communication.

2

One Project Per Category Rule

One specific project can be submitted under one category only. If you enter a full 2BHK under Best Residential Designer, you cannot enter that same flat's kitchen under Best Modular Kitchen Designer.

3

Minimum 6 Real Photos (No AI Allowed)

All project submissions require a minimum of 6 photos (via cloud link OR direct photo upload). Camera-captured finished space photos only (No AI / 3D renders).

4

Max 5 Entries Across Categories

You may enter as many of the 5 categories as you like—up to 5 entries total, each featuring a different project.
